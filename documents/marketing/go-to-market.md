# Go-to-Market Playbook

**Date:** March 19, 2026
**Purpose:** Define the launch strategy, channel priorities, sales motion, and a concrete 90-day action plan for Warehouse Pickup Queue.

## 1. Positioning Statement

> **For warehouse operations managers** who struggle with manual pickup coordination, **Warehouse Pickup Queue** is a **real-time queue management system** that gives customers instant visibility into their pickup status and gate assignment. Unlike generic queue tools or enterprise WMS platforms, our product is **purpose-built for warehouse pickup operations** with native NetSuite integration and a zero-friction mobile experience.

### Supporting Messages

| Audience | Message |
|----------|---------|
| **Operations Manager** | "Stop wasting staff time on 'which gate?' radio calls. Give your customers real-time pickup status and reclaim hours of daily coordination time." |
| **Warehouse Manager** | "Replace your whiteboard with a system your team can learn in 30 minutes. Gate operators see exactly who's next — no more radio chatter." |
| **Branch Manager** | "Your competitors' customers wait in line wondering where to go. Your customers check their phone and know exactly when they're up." |
| **CFO/VP Operations** | "$349/mo saves $1,500/mo in warehouse labor. 4x ROI with a payback period under one week." |

### Channel Details

#### NetSuite Ecosystem (High Priority)

**Why:** Our primary segment (wholesale distributors) over-indexes on NetSuite. The NetSuite integration is our strongest differentiator. This channel provides pre-qualified leads.

**Tactics:**
1. **SuiteApp Marketplace listing** — Submit for Built for NetSuite (BFN) review via the Advanced Partner Center. Requirements: released version, listing review, BFN verification ([Oracle Docs](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1559316827.html)). Target listing by Month 2.
2. **NetSuite user groups** — Attend and present at regional NetSuite user groups (free, high-intent audience).
3. **SuiteWorld conference** — Submit to speak/demo at SuiteWorld (annual Oracle NetSuite conference). Apply early for 2026/2027 event.
4. **NetSuite consultant partnerships** — Offer referral fees (15–20% of first-year revenue) to NetSuite implementation partners who recommend us to their distributor clients.

#### Industry Conferences (High Priority)

**MODEX 2026** — April 13–16, 2026, Georgia World Congress Center, Atlanta ([modexshow.com](https://www.modexshow.com/))
- 1,200+ exhibitors, 600,000 sqft show floor
- Free registration; educational seminars included
- **Action:** Secure booth or shared exhibit space. Demo the Playground app on a large screen. Collect leads via QR code to a trial landing page.
- WERC educational seminars are co-located at MODEX — opportunity for thought leadership presentation.

**ProMat** — Not in 2026 (alternates with MODEX; next ProMat is 2027).

**Other events:** WERC annual conference (warehouse education), industry-specific trade shows (building materials, electrical supply distributor conventions).

#### LinkedIn Outbound (Medium-High Priority)

**Target titles:** Operations Manager, Warehouse Manager, Branch Manager, VP Operations at wholesale distributors with 50–500 employees.

**Approach:**
1. **Connection request** with personalized note referencing their company and industry
2. **Value-first message** — share a relevant insight (e.g., "Most distributors lose 2+ hours/day on pickup coordination — here's how one solved it")
3. **Playground demo invite** — "Want to see what your customers would experience? Try our interactive demo, takes 2 minutes"
4. **Follow-up** — Share ROI calculator or case study

**Volume:** 50–100 targeted connections/week. Expect 5–10% response rate, 1–2% trial conversion from outreach.

## 3. Sales Motion by Tier

### Starter ($149/mo) — Self-Serve

```
Discovery           → Trial              → Conversion         → Onboarding

Google search /     14-day free trial    Credit card           Automated setup
SEO / LinkedIn →    of Professional  →   purchase via      →   wizard + email
                    tier                 website               onboarding sequence
                                                               (5 emails over 14 days)
```

- **No sales call required.** Website sign-up, credit card purchase.
- **Onboarding:** Automated email sequence guiding setup: create account → configure gates → generate QR codes → process first pickup.
- **Upgrade path:** In-app prompts when hitting Starter limits (3 gates, 50 pickups/day). "Upgrade to Professional for unlimited pickups and NetSuite integration."

### Professional ($349/mo) — Inside Sales

```
Discovery           → Demo               → Trial              → Close

LinkedIn outbound / 30-min demo call     1–2 week POC at      Annual or monthly
conference lead /   using Playground →   their facility    →   contract; onboarding
SuiteApp listing    app + live product   with real pickups     call with CS team
```

- **Demo call:** 30 minutes using the Playground app, then switch to the live product. Show gate operator console, NetSuite integration, analytics.
- **POC:** Set up at their facility with real data. Success metric: processing 50+ real pickups during the trial.
- **Close:** Emphasize ROI from trial metrics ("You processed 150 pickups in 5 days — that saved your team ~8 hours of coordination time").
- **Onboarding:** 1-hour kickoff call with customer success. Help configure NetSuite integration, train staff on gate operator console.

- **Scoping:** Involves IT (SSO, security review), Operations (workflow requirements), and Finance (multi-location pricing).
- **Pilot:** 90-day free access at 1–2 locations in exchange for case study rights and expansion commitment.
- **Expand:** Once pilot succeeds, negotiate multi-location annual contract.

## 4. Launch Sequence

### Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1–2)

**Goal:** Establish online presence and begin generating leads.

1. Launch marketing website with pricing page, ROI calculator, and Playground demo link
2. Submit SuiteApp Marketplace listing (BFN review process)
3. Publish 3 SEO-optimized blog posts targeting core keywords
4. Create LinkedIn company page and begin posting weekly content
5. Build email onboarding sequence (5 emails over 14 days)
6. Record 2-minute product walkthrough video
7. Create competitive comparison one-pager

### Phase 2: Outbound (Months 2–3)

**Goal:** Generate first 10–20 trial sign-ups through targeted outreach.

1. Begin LinkedIn outbound campaign (50–100 connections/week)
2. Attend MODEX 2026 (April 13–16) — demo Playground, collect leads
3. Reach out to 10 NetSuite consulting partners
4. Launch 14-day free trial program
5. Publish case study from first pilot customer (or internal dogfooding data)

### Phase 3: Scale (Months 3–6)

**Goal:** Convert trials to paying customers; build repeatable sales motion.

1. Optimize trial-to-paid funnel based on first 20 trials
2. Launch SuiteApp Marketplace listing (if approved)
3. Begin partner referral program with NetSuite consultants
4. Scale LinkedIn outbound to 200+ connections/week (consider hiring SDR)
5. Evaluate paid search for high-intent keywords
6. Publish 2nd and 3rd case studies
